# Actinic Keratosis (AKIEC)

## Overview
Actinic keratosis (AK), also called solar keratosis, is a precancerous skin growth associated with ultraviolet (UV) exposure from sunlight or indoor tanning.

## General educational information
- It may appear as a rough or scaly patch.
- It commonly occurs on areas that receive frequent sun exposure, such as the face, ears, scalp, hands, neck, or lips.
- Some actinic keratoses can develop into squamous cell carcinoma.
- Appearance alone cannot confirm the condition.

## When to seek medical advice
A new, changing, persistent, painful, or bleeding skin lesion should be assessed by a qualified healthcare professional. A dermatologist can examine the lesion and recommend appropriate evaluation.
