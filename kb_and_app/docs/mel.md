# Melanoma (MEL)

## Overview
Melanoma is a type of skin cancer that develops from melanocytes, the cells that produce pigment in the skin. Early medical assessment is important.

## General educational information
- Melanoma can develop in an existing mole or appear as a new spot.
- Warning signs may include changes in size, shape, colour, or appearance.
- Some lesions may have uneven borders or multiple colours.
- Melanoma can sometimes look different from a person's other moles.
- Appearance alone cannot confirm or rule out melanoma.

## When to seek medical advice
Consult a dermatologist promptly if a spot is new, changing, unusual, bleeding, or otherwise concerning. A healthcare professional can determine whether further examination or a biopsy is needed.

## Important limitation
A SkinGuard-AI MEL prediction does not confirm melanoma, and a different prediction does not rule it out. This tool is for educational purposes only, not diagnosis or treatment decisions. Seek professional medical assessment for concerning lesions.
