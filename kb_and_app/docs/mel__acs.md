\# Melanoma: General Information and Warning Signs



\## Overview

Melanoma is a type of skin cancer that develops from pigment-producing cells. Early professional assessment of suspicious skin changes is important.



\## Changes to notice

\- A mole or skin mark may change in size, shape, or colour.

\- One half may look different from the other, or the border may appear irregular.

\- A spot may have multiple colours or look different from a person's other moles.

\- A new or changing skin lesion may need assessment even if it does not cause pain.



\## Important limitation

These features are general warning signs, not a way to diagnose melanoma. Some melanomas do not show every commonly described feature, and benign lesions can sometimes look unusual.



\## When to seek medical advice

Contact a qualified healthcare professional promptly about a new, changing, bleeding, or otherwise suspicious lesion. A clinician can examine it and determine whether further tests are needed. Do not wait for an AI tool to confirm a concern.
