# Vascular Lesions (VASC)

## Overview
Vascular lesions are skin changes associated with blood vessels. They can vary in appearance, size, and cause. This category may include different types of vascular skin conditions.

## General educational information
- Some vascular lesions may appear red, pink, purple, or bluish.
- Their appearance can vary depending on the type of lesion and the blood vessels involved.
- Some are harmless, while others may need medical assessment.
- Different skin conditions can look similar, so appearance alone cannot identify a specific lesion.

## When to seek medical advice
Consult a healthcare professional if a lesion is new, changing, painful, bleeding, or otherwise concerning. Seek prompt medical advice for unexplained or persistent changes.

## Important limitation
A SkinGuard-AI VASC prediction does not confirm a specific vascular condition or guarantee that a lesion is harmless. The model may misclassify images. Consult a dermatologist for assessment.
