\# Actinic Keratosis: Symptoms and Professional Assessment



\## Overview

Actinic keratosis is a skin condition associated with damage from ultraviolet radiation. It may develop after repeated exposure to sunlight over time.



\## Possible features

\- Affected skin may feel rough, dry, or scaly.

\- The area may have a different colour from the surrounding skin.

\- Changes may occur on commonly sun-exposed areas, such as the face, ears, scalp, and hands.

\- Other skin conditions can have similar features, so visual appearance alone cannot confirm the cause.



\## General skin protection

Reducing excessive UV exposure is an important preventive measure. Shade, protective clothing, and sunscreen can help protect exposed skin. These steps do not determine whether an existing lesion is harmless.



\## When to seek medical advice

A persistent, growing, changing, painful, or bleeding skin lesion should be assessed by a qualified healthcare professional. Seek professional guidance rather than trying to identify a lesion using an image or an AI prediction alone.
