\# Common Moles and Atypical Nevi: General Information



\## Overview

A common mole, also called a melanocytic nevus, is a growth formed by pigment-producing cells in the skin. Moles vary in colour, size, and shape. Some are present from birth, while others appear later.



\## Changes to notice

\- A mole may change in size, shape, colour, or surface.

\- A spot that looks noticeably different from a person's other moles deserves attention.

\- Atypical-looking moles may have irregular features, but appearance alone cannot determine whether a lesion is cancerous.

\- Not every mole becomes melanoma, and not every melanoma develops from a pre-existing mole.



\## When to seek medical advice

A new or changing mole, an unusual spot, or a lesion that bleeds or causes persistent concern should be assessed by a qualified healthcare professional. A clinician can decide whether examination or further testing is needed.



\## SkinGuard-AI limitation

SkinGuard-AI is an educational image-classification tool, not a diagnostic system. It cannot confirm that a mole is benign or rule out melanoma. Do not delay professional assessment because of its prediction.
