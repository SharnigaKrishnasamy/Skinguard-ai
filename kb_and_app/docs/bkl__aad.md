\# Seborrheic Keratosis: General Information



\## Overview

Seborrheic keratosis is a common, usually benign skin growth. It becomes more frequent with age and can occur as one growth or several.



\## Possible features

\- A growth may look raised, waxy, rough, or wart-like.

\- Colours can range from light tan and brown to darker shades.

\- Growths commonly appear on the head, neck, chest, back, or other areas of the body.

\- Some lesions may resemble other skin conditions, including certain precancerous growths or skin cancers.



\## Important distinction

An image or visual description cannot reliably establish that a growth is harmless. A clinician may need to examine an unusual or changing lesion and decide whether further investigation is appropriate.



\## When to seek medical advice

Seek professional assessment if a growth changes noticeably, grows quickly, bleeds, becomes persistently irritated, or looks different from other growths. Do not attempt to remove a skin growth yourself.
