# Basal Cell Carcinoma (BCC)

## Overview
Basal cell carcinoma is a type of skin cancer that begins in basal cells of the skin. It is commonly associated with long-term ultraviolet (UV) exposure.

## General educational information
- It may appear as a shiny bump, a pink growth, or a sore that heals and returns.
- It can sometimes bleed or develop a crust.
- It commonly appears on sun-exposed areas, including the face and neck.
- A skin lesion's appearance alone cannot confirm basal cell carcinoma.

## When to seek medical advice
Seek assessment from a qualified healthcare professional for a persistent, growing, bleeding, or non-healing skin lesion. A dermatologist may recommend an examination or biopsy.

## Important limitation
SkinGuard-AI provides an image-classification prediction, not a medical diagnosis. Do not use the result alone to determine whether a lesion is cancerous. Consult a dermatologist for assessment.
