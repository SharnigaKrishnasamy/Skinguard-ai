# Benign Keratosis-Like Lesions (BKL)

## Overview
BKL is a broad image-classification category used in the SkinGuard-AI project. It includes benign keratosis-like appearances; this label alone does not identify one specific skin condition.

## General educational information
- Seborrhoeic keratoses are common, non-cancerous skin growths.
- They may look waxy, rough, or as if they are stuck onto the skin.
- Their colour and size can vary.
- Other skin lesions can look similar, so appearance alone cannot confirm the diagnosis.

## When to seek medical advice
Ask a healthcare professional to assess a new or changing lesion, or one that itches, hurts, bleeds, or looks different from your other spots.

## Important limitation
A SkinGuard-AI BKL prediction does not confirm that a lesion is benign. The model may misclassify images. Consult a dermatologist for an appropriate assessment.
