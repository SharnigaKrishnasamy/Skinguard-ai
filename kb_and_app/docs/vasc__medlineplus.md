# Hemangioma — MedlinePlus Summary

## Overview
A hemangioma is a growth involving blood vessels. Hemangiomas may appear as red, purple, or other visibly coloured areas on the skin. Their appearance and course can vary.

## Common Features
- A hemangioma may be visible as a raised or flat skin mark.
- Appearance depends on the type, size, and depth of the blood-vessel growth.
- Not every red or purple skin lesion is a hemangioma.

## Assessment and Safety
A skin image or automated classifier cannot reliably confirm that a lesion is a hemangioma. A healthcare professional should assess lesions that are changing, bleeding, painful, ulcerated, or otherwise concerning. Seek urgent medical advice for significant bleeding or other severe symptoms.
