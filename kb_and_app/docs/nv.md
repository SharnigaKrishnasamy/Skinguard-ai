# Melanocytic Nevi (NV)

## Overview
Melanocytic nevi, commonly called moles, are common skin growths formed by pigment-producing cells called melanocytes.

## General educational information
- Moles can be flat or raised.
- Their colour may vary, including shades of brown, black, or skin colour.
- Some moles are present from birth, while others develop over time.
- A changing or unusual mole should not automatically be assumed to be harmless.
- Appearance alone cannot confirm the nature of a skin lesion.

## When to seek medical advice
Consult a dermatologist if a mole changes in size, shape, or colour, develops irregular borders, bleeds, or looks noticeably different from your other moles.
