\# Dermatofibroma: General Information



\## Overview

A dermatofibroma is a common, usually harmless skin growth. It may develop after a minor skin injury, although the exact cause is not always clear.



\## Possible features

\- It often feels firm and may be small and rounded.

\- Its colour can range from pink or brown to a darker shade.

\- It commonly appears on the legs, but can occur elsewhere.

\- The appearance of a dermatofibroma can overlap with other skin conditions, so self-identification is not always reliable.



\## Monitoring

Avoid picking, cutting, or attempting to remove a skin growth at home. A healthcare professional can examine a lesion if its identity is uncertain.



\## When to seek medical advice

Ask a qualified healthcare professional to assess a lesion that changes, grows, bleeds, becomes painful, or has an unusual appearance. A clinician can decide whether further investigation is needed.
