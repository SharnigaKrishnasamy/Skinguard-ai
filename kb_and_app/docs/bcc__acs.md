\# Basal Cell Carcinoma: General Information



\## Overview

Basal cell carcinoma (BCC) is a common type of skin cancer that begins in basal cells of the skin. Ultraviolet radiation from sunlight or tanning devices is an important risk factor.



\## Possible features

\- It may appear as a shiny or pearly bump.

\- Some lesions may look like a flat, scaly, reddish area.

\- A sore may bleed, crust, heal, and then return.

\- Appearance varies, and other skin conditions may look similar.



\## Risk reduction

Reducing excessive UV exposure may help lower skin-cancer risk. Shade, protective clothing, and broad-spectrum sunscreen are useful general protective measures. These measures cannot determine the nature of an existing lesion.



\## When to seek medical advice

A new, persistent, growing, bleeding, or changing skin lesion should be evaluated by a qualified healthcare professional. A dermatologist can examine it and decide whether further assessment is needed.
