# Dermatofibroma (DF)

## Overview
Dermatofibroma is a common, usually harmless skin growth. It can develop after minor skin injuries, although the exact cause is not always known.

## General educational information
- It often appears as a small, firm bump on the skin.
- Its colour may range from pink or brown to darker shades.
- It commonly occurs on the legs but can appear on other parts of the body.
- Other skin conditions can look similar, so appearance alone cannot confirm dermatofibroma.

## When to seek medical advice
Consult a healthcare professional if a skin lesion is new, growing, changing colour, painful, or bleeding, or if you are unsure what it is.

## Important limitation
A SkinGuard-AI DF prediction is an image-classification result, not a medical diagnosis. Do not rely on it alone to identify a skin growth. Consult a dermatologist for assessment.
